# Content budget

Hard caps. Raise a number only when the current cap is fun.

## Maps

| Tier | Authored vocabularies | Maps per run |
|---|---|---|
| County | 1 lot (exists) | 1 |
| Town | 2 templates | 1 rolled |
| City | 2 templates | 1 rolled |
| Capitol | 1 fixed | 1 |

No more than **6 maps** in 1.0. Variety is layout shuffle + target order, not a hundred unique lots.

## Buildings

Max **8 prefabs** for 1.0: shack, brick office, yard, plant, concrete block, garage, steel mid-rise, tower floor.

## Threats

Max **6:** cruiser, jersey/dump blocker, excavator, fire truck, wagon/bus, chopper.

## Attachments

Max **5:** ripper, wide blade, wrecking ball, pile driver, extra plate.

## Meta unlocks

Max **8** total. If a unlock does not change collapse or stuck, cut it.

## Art (when you generate any)

- One 16px tileset expansion per tier, not a new style
- One dozer body + facing set
- One unit sheet
- No unique character portraits
- Placeholder rects remain legal until a map is fun

## Audio

Engine, scrape, three impacts, one collapse, one siren. Then stop.

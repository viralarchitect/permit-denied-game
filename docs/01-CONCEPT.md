# PERMIT DENIED — Concept

**Title:** PERMIT DENIED  
**Inspiration:** armored dozer vs a town. No real names. No manifesto.  
**Form:** top-down tank-steer destruction roguelike. One life per run.  
**Stack lock:** Go + Ebitengine, 320×224, nearest-neighbor. Do not reopen.

## Pitch

You drive an armored bulldozer through a county that said no. Buildings fall. Cars bounce. The map gets bigger. The dozer does not get faster. It gets heavier. The run ends at the governor’s penthouse or with the machine stuck.

## Player fantasy

Unstoppable weight. Targeted demolition. Resistance that arrives and fails.

## Win / lose

- **Win a map:** listed targets are rubble.
- **Win a run:** penthouse floor is rubble or the tower falls with it.
- **Lose:** immobilized (stuck, buried, heat-killed) or timer expires on a timed map.

## Session

15–25 minutes for a full run. County map alone is the short session (~3–5 min).

## Tone

Dry. Official. Petty. The UI talks like a denied permit, not a trailer. No speech, no cutscenes, no biography.

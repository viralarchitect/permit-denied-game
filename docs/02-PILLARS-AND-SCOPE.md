# Pillars and scope

## Pillars

1. **Weight over speed.** Turning costs. Hits feel expensive. Boosts are rare and short.
2. **Targeted demolition.** Named buildings, not sandbox graffiti. You always know the next structure that matters.
3. **Resistance that cannot stop you — only stall you.** Cars, cruisers, blockers, choppers exist to waste time and pin you, not to win a DPS fight.
4. **One machine.** Attachments change how you break things. You never leave the dozer.

## In

- Four tiers: County → Towns → City → Capitol
- Procedural layout inside authored tile vocabularies
- Per-run attachments + between-run meta unlocks
- Rubble with mass (maze and ramp)
- Heat / immobilization as the fail state

## Out (non-goals)

- Real people, real town, real incident
- Title change to KILLDOZER
- Guns as the main verb
- Open world, missions, dialogue, cutscenes
- Multiplayer, accounts, live ops
- Soft-body destruction, 3D, new engine
- On-foot sections, second vehicle, eject
- Satire that needs a lecture to land

## Scope law

County must be fun before Town exists. Town must be fun before City exists. Capitol is last. No parallel content tracks.
